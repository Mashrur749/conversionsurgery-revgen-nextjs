# 09 — Offer Mastery

## What this is

You read two documents cover-to-cover and absorb the offer + ICP until you can recite the core moves without looking. Memorization isn't the goal — internalization is. The language has to come out of your mouth naturally on a sales call, not get pulled from notes.

## Before you start this

- [ ] Platform rehearsal complete (you know the product works)
- [ ] Quiet 45 minutes — no Slack, no email, phone face-down

## Time required

~1.5 hours

## Why this matters

You'll be reciting this language during sales calls. If you stumble on the offer, the price, or the guarantee, contractors stop trusting you. They expect the seller to know the product better than they do. Read it once now so you never have to read it during a call.

## What you'll do

### 1. Read OFFER-APPROVED-COPY end to end (~30 min)

Open `docs/business-intel/OFFER-APPROVED-COPY.md`. Read every section in order. Don't skim. Pay extra attention to:

- §1 Positioning & Dream Outcome — the exact phrasing you use in proposals vs. verbal
- §2 Core Service Description — what's included, what's not
- §3 Guarantee — the operational guarantee in its approved form
- §4 Pricing — Pilot vs. Standard, setup split, monthly start
- §5 Service Terms & Cancellation — Day-14 right, 90-day minimum, 30-day pause
- §10 Common Questions — these are the FAQ answers verbatim

### 2. Read ICP-DEFINITION end to end (~15 min)

Open `docs/business-intel/ICP-DEFINITION.md`. Read every section in order. Pay extra attention to:

- The 30-Second Qualifier (the 6 questions)
- Sub-Niches (Priority Order)
- Where to Find Them (Calgary + Edmonton)
- Cold Call Script
- Who to AVOID

### 3. Self-test (~30 min)

Close both docs. Open a blank text file. Write down — without looking — the following. If you can't, re-read until you can.

#### The offer in 2 sentences

A clear, plain-English statement of what you sell and the dream outcome.

#### The 3 tiers + pricing

- Pilot: $3,500 setup + $1,500/mo (first 3 clients, 90-day min)
- Standard: $5,500 setup + $2,000/mo (client 4+)
- Setup split 50/50, Day-7 non-refundable, monthly starts at go-live

#### The operational guarantee in your own words

The 21-day go-live commitment, the 30-day logging threshold, what happens if either misses.

#### The 6-question qualifier from ICP

The full 30-Second Qualifier — every question, in order.

#### Top 3 sub-niches in priority order

From ICP §"Sub-Niches (Priority Order)".

#### The cold call hook

The exact opening line from ICP §"Cold Call Script". Word-for-word.

#### The 4 Irresistibility Levers

The four levers from E2E §2.4.5. Name them and the one-line value of each.

### 4. Re-read what you got wrong

For every blank or fuzzy answer, go back to the doc and read that section again. Then redo the self-test on those items only.

## What success looks like

- [ ] OFFER-APPROVED-COPY read in full
- [ ] ICP-DEFINITION read in full
- [ ] Self-test complete with all 7 items written from memory
- [ ] You can answer "what do you do?" in 2 sentences without thinking

## If something goes wrong

If a phrase in OFFER-APPROVED-COPY conflicts with your understanding from earlier docs — flag it, don't silently override. OFFER-APPROVED-COPY is the source of truth. Per CLAUDE.md, do not edit that doc without asking. Note the discrepancy in `.scratch/offer-questions.md` and resolve later.
