# 10 — Script Rehearsal (Out Loud)

## What this is

You read the sales scripts out loud, then run one mock discovery call with a friend or family member playing the contractor. Then a second mock to smooth the rough edges.

## Before you start this

- [ ] Offer mastery complete (you've internalized the offer + ICP)
- [ ] A friend or family member who can give you 30 minutes to roleplay
- [ ] Phone or laptop with a voice recorder app
- [ ] Quiet room with a door

## Time required

~1.75 hours

## Why out loud

Silent reading lands at roughly 30% retention. Reading aloud gets you to ~70%. Running a mock call where you have to respond to a live human gets you to ~90%. Skipping the mock call cuts the value of this step in half — don't.

## What you'll do

### Step 1: Read aloud (45 min)

Read each of the following at speaking pace, out loud, alone in the room. Don't whisper. Don't mumble. Speak it the way you'd say it on a real call.

- **COLD-START-PLAYBOOK Scripts A through G** — every cold-outreach script you'll use
- **E2E §2.1** — cold call openers (the one you memorized in file 09)
- **E2E §2.4.5** — the Four Irresistibility Levers in full, all four
- **E2E §2.5** — the Demo Close script
- **02-MANAGED-SERVICE-PLAYBOOK §1.7** — Dead Lead Resurrection demo prep (Lever 1)
- **02-MANAGED-SERVICE-PLAYBOOK §1.8** — Spouse Line protocol Q&A (Lever 4)
- **SALES-OBJECTION-PLAYBOOK** — skim all 14 objections (you have 13 numbered + 3.5 gatekeeper). Don't memorize answers. Just know which objection lives where so you can flip to it mid-call.

After reading, jot down the 2-3 phrases that felt unnatural in your mouth. These are the ones you rewrite in your own voice before the mock call — the script is a starting point, not a script-script.

### Step 2: Mock discovery call #1 (45 min)

Set up your mock call partner with a one-paragraph brief:

> You're the owner of a $2M Calgary basement renovation company. You do ~20 leads/month, your spouse handles the office line, you book maybe 35% of estimates, and you've been thinking lately that leads slip through the cracks but you don't have data. You're skeptical of "AI" and you've been burned by a marketing agency before. Be a real human — push back, ask questions, throw an objection or two.

Then run the full discovery call:

1. Cold opener (E2E §2.1)
2. Discovery questions / 10-question scorecard (E2E §2.2)
3. Tier recommendation (E2E §2.3)
4. Insert the Four Irresistibility Levers (E2E §2.4.5) BEFORE quoting price
5. Quote the price
6. Handle whatever objection lands (route via SALES-OBJECTION-PLAYBOOK Quick Reference)
7. Demo Close (E2E §2.5)

**Record the audio on your phone.** You will not catch your own filler words and stumble points without playback. This is the entire point.

After the call, listen back. Mark down:

- Where you stumbled (specific timestamps)
- Where you sounded canned or read-from-page
- Where you missed a Lever or skipped a question
- Where the objection threw you

### Step 3: Mock discovery call #2 (15 min)

Run it again with the same partner. Different objection if they can manage one. Smoother delivery on the rough spots from call #1. You don't need to record this one — you'll feel the difference.

## What success looks like

- [ ] All listed scripts read aloud once
- [ ] Mock call #1 completed and recorded
- [ ] Audio reviewed, stumble points noted
- [ ] Mock call #2 completed and noticeably smoother

## If something goes wrong

If your mock partner can't make it — call a different friend, or as a fallback record yourself running both sides of the call (you-as-contractor first, then you-as-operator responding). Less ideal than a real human but better than skipping.

If a Lever doesn't land in the mock — go back to E2E §2.4.5 and 02-MANAGED-SERVICE-PLAYBOOK §1.7/§1.8. The Levers are the single biggest leverage move in the script. They cannot be soft.
