# 12 — Day 2 Checkpoint

## What this is

End-of-Day-2 verification. Ten boxes to clear before you start outreach on Day 3. If any box is unchecked, do not skip ahead — finish the box first.

## Before you start this

- [ ] Files 08-11 complete

## Time required

~15 minutes (this is verification, not work)

## What you'll do

Walk through the checklist below. Be honest with yourself. Half-done counts as not done.

### Platform proven

- [ ] Test contractor (Peak Basements YYC) signed up via wizard, walked the full E2E in test mode end to end
- [ ] All 16 production tests pass against the test client (E2E §6.1-6.16)
- [ ] Day-21 go-live gate verified manually (E2E §6.13)
- [ ] Day-30 logging gate verified manually (E2E §6.14)
- [ ] Day-14 cancel form tested — confirmed row written to `client_cancellations` and Stripe subscription cancelled per the offer math

### Offer mastered

- [ ] `docs/business-intel/OFFER-APPROVED-COPY.md` read in full
- [ ] `docs/business-intel/ICP-DEFINITION.md` read in full
- [ ] Self-test passed — you can recite, without notes: the offer in 2 sentences, the 3 tiers + pricing, the operational guarantee, the 6-question qualifier, the top 3 sub-niches, and the 4 Irresistibility Levers

### Sales motion rehearsed

- [ ] Mock discovery call recorded and reviewed (audio playback done, stumble points noted)
- [ ] Loom Pro account active + "Audit Template v1" recorded and saved
- [ ] `calgary-basement-prospects.csv` at 30+ qualified rows with all required columns and ICP exclusion gates applied

## What you have at end of Day 2

- A platform you've personally walked end-to-end. You know it works because you broke it and fixed it (or watched it not break).
- An offer you can recite in your sleep — no fumbling for price, no hesitation on the guarantee.
- A sales motion you've rehearsed out loud, with one recorded mock call as proof.
- 30+ named prospects ready for outreach Day 3.

## What's left for Day 3

- Send the first batch of personalized cold emails (per COLD-START-PLAYBOOK weekly outreach floor)
- Build the first 3 Dead Lead Resurrection demos for any warm replies that come back
- Continue ongoing reactive lifecycle (calls, demos, follow-ups) once leads start moving

## A2P / 10DLC status check

If your A2P/10DLC registration is approved — go to `/admin/agency` and associate the production Twilio number now. New SMS will route through the registered campaign immediately.

If A2P is still pending — continue with Day 3 outreach (cold email + cold calls don't depend on A2P). Associate the Twilio number the moment approval lands. Test SMS flow end-to-end again on the live number once associated.

## If something goes wrong

If you have any unchecked boxes — finish them before Day 3 outreach. The cost of going to market half-prepared is dramatically higher than the cost of one more day of prep. Outreach with an unproven platform burns prospects you can't easily re-approach.
